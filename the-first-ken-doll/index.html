The first Ken doll

23 March 202623 March 20261 min read

Two years after Barbie’s release and success, Mattel decided to release a male counterpart to Barbie. Ken Sean Carson, named after Ruth’s son, was created in 1961 after complaints about Barbie’s “singledom” came to light. Ken stands two inches taller then Barbie and also comes with a set of dapper clothing.
